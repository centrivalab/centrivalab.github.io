Rotor Speed Calculator

The rotor speed that gives a target pressure in a CentrivaLab cartridge — or the pressure you get at a given speed. Because the free surface recedes as permeate is collected, ΔP falls during a run at constant speed: enter the initial feed volume and the permeate you intend to collect, and the speed is set for the mean pressure between the start and the end of the run.

The ◀ row is your operating point. Red rows exceed the rotor's maximum speed. Speeds are rounded to 10 rpm; most centrifuges accept 10 or 100 rpm steps.

Model: ΔP = ½ρω²(rm² − rs²), with rm = rmax − δm the membrane radius and rs(V) = rmax − δs(V) the free-surface radius from the cartridge calibration (94/25: measured over 7.4–22.8 mL; 50/34: 11–13 mL). rmax is the rotor's maximum radius as given by the manufacturer. Over a run the speed is chosen for the arithmetic mean of ΔP at V0 and at V0 − Vp, which is within ~0.3 bar of the time-averaged pressure for Vp up to about half of V0.
