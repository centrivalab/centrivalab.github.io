Model: Geraldes V., Fan H., Yang M., Rodrigues M., Elimelech M. — "Predicting concentration polarization in Ortho-Centrifugal Membrane Filters for NF/RO membrane screening." Preprint under review, Journal of Membrane Science (2026). Thermo-physical properties at 20 °C from Table 1 of the same work. Correlation kc/U* = 0.28 𝒢1/4 Sc−1/4, validated for Sc ∈ [700, 2200] and 𝒢 ∈ [7×10⁻⁹, 5×10⁻⁶]. Mean relative deviation ~7%, maximum ~22% vs DNS data. UF model (PEG 35 kDa): the same law with its prefactor re-extracted from the DNS with the finite-permeation closure of Geraldes & Afonso (2006), kc/U* = 0.261 𝒢1/4 Sc−1/4 and Γ = Rint φX / (1 + (1 − Rint) φX), X = (1 + 0.26 φ1.4)1.7, replacing film theory (valid only for φ ≲ 1.5); in the O-CMF, ν and D evaluated at the film concentration ½(cm + c0). In the stirred cell and the spiral-wound module (forced convection) the correlations keep the bulk properties and kc is multiplied by the weak viscosity correction (νm/ν0)−0.075 found in the reconstructed radial cross-flow cell (36 runs); evaluating a Re0.875Sc0.25 law at the film concentration would over-correct. Validated on twelve reconstructed PEG 35 kDa ultrafiltration experiments in the O-CMF (Pereira, MSc thesis, IST 2026; Sc ≈ 3×10⁴, Γ 2–66, φ 1.4–7, within ±20 % in Γ). PEG 35 kDa property laws at 20 °C (Table A.1 of the same work; data of Pires et al.): quartic ν(c) and DA(c), linear density, cubic osmotic pressure. The UF membrane classes are indicative permeabilities; B defaults to the value backed out of the reconstructions (Rint ≈ 0.96–0.996).
