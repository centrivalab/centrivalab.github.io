CentrivaLab 50/34

Current public reference configuration for the improved O–CMF–50mL–34deg cartridge.

Configuration overview

O–CMF–50mL–34deg

Orthogonal centrifugal membrane filter cartridge for fixed-angle centrifuge rotors designed around standard high-speed 50 mL round-bottom polycarbonate tubes.

Centrifugal pressure model. The transmembrane pressure generated at rotor angular velocity ω is

Δp = ½ ρ ω² ( rm² − rs² )

where the radial positions of the membrane (rm) and of the liquid free surface (rs) are referred to the rotor outer radius rmax via two cartridge-geometry constants and the fill volume V:

rm = rmax − δm     rs = rmax − ( δs* + αV )

Nominal feed volume13.0 mL
Operating fill range11–13 mL
Membrane chamber volume4.6 mL
Membrane coupon diameter20 mm
Effective membrane area1.45 cm²
Membrane offset, δm  (distance rmax − rm)0.0318 m
Free-surface intercept, δs*  (distance rmax − rs at V = 0)0.0367 m
Free-surface slope, α  (rate at which rs moves toward the axis as V increases)0.00160 m mL−1

Cartridge geometry

Cross-sectional reference showing the radial coordinates used in the centrifugal pressure model.

Design features

Open membrane chamber

Internal supports were removed to create a fully open membrane chamber.

Improved capacity

The revised cartridge increases membrane chamber volume to 4.6 mL.

Improved membrane area

The effective membrane area is increased to 1.45 cm², with sealing provided by a compressed elastomeric O-ring.
