CentrivaLab 94/25

Orthogonal Centrifugal Membrane Filter cartridge for fixed-angle rotors accommodating 94 mL round-bottom tubes at ~25°.

Nominal feed volume 22.5 mL
Operating fill range 21.5–22.5 mL
Max pressure (Sigma 12159) ~40 bar
Rotor speed limit 15 500 rpm

Cartridge description

The CentrivaLab 94/25 is an orthogonal centrifugal membrane filter (O-CMF) cartridge that converts rotor speed directly into transmembrane pressure without external pumps. The membrane is oriented orthogonally to the centrifugal acceleration field so that pressure acts normal to the membrane surface, thereby minimising concentration polarisation.

The CentrivaLab 94/25 is intended for use in fixed-angle centrifuge rotors accommodating standard round-bottom centrifuge tubes of 94 mL, with a rotor angle of approximately 25°. The reference rotor is the Sigma fixed-angle rotor 12159 (6 × 94 mL, 25°, maximum speed 15 500 rpm).

The radial coordinates used throughout this specification are the membrane radius \(r_m\), the free-surface radius \(r_s(V)\), and the rotor-imposed maximum liquid radius \(r_{\max}\). Their definitions and the cartridge geometry are shown in Figure 1.

Free-surface position and centrifugal pressure

All radial positions are expressed relative to the rotor-imposed maximum liquid radius \(r_{\max}\). The membrane and free-surface offsets are defined as

$$\delta_m = r_{\max} - r_m, \qquad \delta_s = r_{\max} - r_s .$$

Based on direct geometric measurements, the free-surface position follows a linear correlation with fill volume \(V\) (in mL):

$$\delta_s(V) = \delta_s^{\ast} + \alpha V ,$$

giving the free-surface radius

$$r_s(V) = r_{\max} - \delta_s(V) .$$

Under rigid-body rotation, the radial hydrostatic balance yields the transmembrane pressure:

$$\Delta p = \frac{\rho\,\omega^2}{2}\!\left[r_m^2 - r_s(V)^2\right],$$

where \(\omega = 2\pi N/60\) and \(N\) is rotor speed in RPM. Expressed in bar:

$$\Delta p_{\rm bar}(V,N) = C_0\, N^2 \left[r_m^2 - r_s(V)^2\right],$$

with the constant

$$C_0 = \frac{\rho}{2\times10^5}\!\left(\frac{2\pi}{60}\right)^{\!2} \approx 5.48\times10^{-5}$$

Valid for water at 20 °C.

Application to the Sigma rotor 12159

For the Sigma fixed-angle rotor 12159, the geometric parameters are:

$$r_{\max} = 0.099\ \text{m}, \qquad r_m = 0.0722\ \text{m}.$$

The maximum achievable transmembrane pressure at 15 500 rpm is approximately 40 bar at nominal fill volume.

Empirical correlations for the Sigma rotor 12159

The free-surface radius for \(V \in [21.5,\,22.5]\) mL:

$$r_s(V) = 0.099 - \!\left(2.384\times10^{-2} + 1.315\times10^{-3}\,V\right) \quad [\text{m}]$$

Transmembrane pressure directly in bar, with \(N\) in RPM:

$$\Delta p_{\rm bar}(V,N) = 5.48\times10^{-5}\,N^2 \left[0.0722^2 - \left(0.07516 - 1.315\times10^{-3}\,V\right)^2\right]$$

where \(0.07516 = r_{\max} - \delta_s^{\ast} = 0.099 - 0.02384\) m.

Inverse relation — rotor speed required for a target pressure:

$$N(V,\Delta p_{\rm bar}) = \sqrt{\frac{\Delta p_{\rm bar}}{5.48\times10^{-5}\left[0.0722^2 - \left(0.07516 - 1.315\times10^{-3}\,V\right)^2\right]}}$$
