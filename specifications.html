Materials

Construction materials

The cartridge body can be manufactured in different engineering materials depending on the chemical compatibility required.

  • Acrylic resin for standard laboratory use
  • PEEK / PAEK for solvent-oriented workflows
  • PPS for solvent-oriented workflows

PEEK, PAEK and PPS are recommended for OSN applications.

Rotor compatibility — CentrivaLab 50/34

Fixed-angle rotors for 50 mL tubes

  • Beckman Coulter JA-20 (6 × 50 mL)
  • Beckman Coulter JA-20.50 (6 × 50 mL)
  • Beckman Coulter JA-25.50 (6 × 50 mL)

Exact operating conditions depend on rotor geometry and centrifuge model.

Rotor compatibility — CentrivaLab 94/25

Fixed-angle rotors for 94–100 mL tubes

  • Sigma 12159 (6 × 94 mL, 25°, 15 500 rpm)
  • Sigma 12155 (4 × 94 mL, 26°, 20 000 rpm)
  • Thermo / Heraeus F15-6×100 (6 × 100 mL, 25°, 15 000 rpm)
  • Thermo / Heraeus HFA 15.9 (6 × 94 mL, 26°, 10 500–12 000 rpm)

Exact operating conditions depend on rotor geometry and centrifuge model.
