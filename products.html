Product detail

CentrivaLab cartridge

The single-use cartridge houses a flat membrane disc. After centrifugation, retentate and permeate are collected separately for analysis. The numbered cap system ensures sample tracking across parallel experiments.

Construction materials

Cartridge bodies are available in different engineering materials depending on the chemical compatibility required by the application.

Acrylic resin

Standard option for aqueous NF and RO applications. Suitable for most laboratory membrane screening workflows.

PEEK / PAEK

Recommended for organic solvent nanofiltration (OSN) applications requiring resistance to a broad range of organic solvents.

PPS

Alternative high-performance polymer for solvent-oriented workflows where PEEK/PAEK compatibility needs to be confirmed case by case.
